# Code of Conduct

KARADAVI is intended to be a rigorous, respectful research environment.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- communicate respectfully;
- challenge ideas without attacking people;
- distinguish evidence, assumptions, and opinions;
- welcome technically grounded disagreement; and
- keep discussions focused on improving the work.

Harassment, personal attacks, deliberate misinformation, and disclosure of private or sensitive information are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove contributions or discussions that violate these expectations.
