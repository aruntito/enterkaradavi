# Glossary

A small shared vocabulary keeps KARADAVI discussions precise.

| Term | Working meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Observation** | Something a system has directly received or recorded. |
| **Evidence** | An observation or source material used to support or challenge a claim. |
| **Claim** | A proposition asserted about an entity, event, artifact, or relationship. |
| **Provenance** | Information describing where evidence or a claim came from and how it was obtained or transformed. |
| **Authority** | Context-dependent standing a source has for establishing or informing a particular claim. |
| **Uncertainty** | Explicit representation of what is unknown, ambiguous, incomplete, or weakly supported. |
| **Trust signal** | A structured machine-readable interpretation of evidence and context. |
| **Entity resolution** | The process of determining whether different observations refer to the same underlying entity. |
| **Semantic state** | A structured representation of what a system currently believes or can support about an entity or claim. |
| **Context** | The conditions under which a claim, source, or authority relationship is evaluated. |

These are working definitions, not immutable standards. Changes should be documented when research shows that a term is too broad or ambiguous.
