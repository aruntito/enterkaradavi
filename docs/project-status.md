# Project Status

**Project:** KARADAVI — The Knowledge Forest  
**Product direction:** Open knowledge platform / continuously evolving knowledge institution  
**Public repository:** Research and specification companion  
**Canonical product repository:** Private — `aruntito/karadavi`

This page distinguishes the maturity of the public research repository from the broader product roadmap. The existence of a public specification does not mean every product capability described by the vision is implemented.

## Product vision

KARADAVI aims to make meaningful knowledge explorable as a connected, evidence-aware and continuously maintained system. The ten knowledge trails are initial discovery surfaces over an extensible entity model.

The long-term institution is broader than the first release. Launch scope is a reliable end-to-end knowledge path, not mass population of every domain.

## Product foundations reported as implemented

The following foundations have been reported in the product's development roadmap:

- project and core database foundations;
- canonical entity engine;
- ten knowledge-domain discovery trails;
- assisted source extraction pipeline;
- structured draft storage;
- AI-assisted drafting with human publication authority;
- initial admin/editorial workflow foundations;
- initial public entity experience foundations;
- entity type alias normalization;
- PWA installation foundations;
- origin-story experience.

These are product progress notes, not a claim that every production workflow is fully hardened or that all planned knowledge operations are complete. The canonical implementation remains private.

## Product work still identified for completion

- deepen CMS and editorial workflows;
- expand and validate entity relationships;
- build knowledge graph traversal;
- harden provenance, evidence and claim lifecycle semantics;
- complete production hardening and editorial quality assurance;
- strengthen administrative security, roles, auditability and operational controls;
- mature research and update workflows;
- improve public discovery and machine-readable knowledge outputs.

## Intended continuous-stewardship capabilities

The product direction includes manual research, source discovery and validation, extraction and normalization, entity resolution, source reconciliation, evidence/provenance tracking, conflict detection, stale-knowledge detection, and autonomous discovery/update proposals.

These capabilities must remain distinguishable by maturity. Planned automation does not imply autonomous publication.

## Editorial invariant

> **AI never publishes.**

AI may discover, extract, normalize, compare, draft and propose. Human editorial review and explicit approval remain mandatory before a change becomes published knowledge.

## Public research repository status

This repository provides the public conceptual architecture, research program, terminology, design principles, experimental schemas, reference examples, documentation, contribution guidance and automated validation.

Its current research foundation is organized around identity, evidence, authority, uncertainty and trust. The next research work should turn those concepts into evidence-backed candidate specifications through reproducible experiments and documented failure cases.

## Status vocabulary

- **Implemented:** verified as part of the current product or public repository.
- **Partially implemented:** a foundation exists, but the complete workflow is not finished or hardened.
- **Planned:** an accepted direction or roadmap item not yet verified as complete.
- **Aspirational:** a long-term capability or outcome, not a near-term delivery claim.

## Scope note

This public repository does not expose private implementation status beyond high-level, maintainer-reported progress. It does not contain production source code, private datasets, deployment configuration or internal operational tooling.
