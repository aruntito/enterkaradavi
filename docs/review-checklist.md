# Review Checklist

For reviewers evaluating a public KARADAVI change:

## Correctness

- Does the change match the stated problem?
- Are observations, claims, and interpretations separated?
- Are edge cases and conflicting evidence considered?

## Specification quality

- Is terminology precise?
- Are semantics explicit?
- Is compatibility impact clear?
- Are examples sufficient?

## Research quality

- Is there evidence or a clearly stated hypothesis?
- Are limitations documented?
- Could another contributor reproduce the reasoning?

## Public boundary

- Does the change avoid private implementation and operational information?
- Are credentials, personal data, and private datasets absent?

## Maintainability

- Is the change focused?
- Does documentation point to the right next step?
- Does validation cover the affected artifact?
