# Research Quality

KARADAVI is a research project, so repository activity should not be confused with research progress.

## Evidence levels

### Idea

A proposed concept with a clear question but no supporting experiment.

### Hypothesis

A falsifiable claim with stated assumptions and a proposed test.

### Observed

An experiment has produced a documented observation.

### Reproduced

The observation has been repeated under documented conditions.

### Candidate

The evidence is strong enough to inform a specification proposal.

### Stable

The concept has survived compatibility, edge-case, and failure-mode review.

## Quality rules

- Prefer small reproducible experiments over broad unsupported claims.
- Separate observations from interpretations.
- Record contradictory evidence.
- Make uncertainty visible.
- State what the experiment cannot establish.
- Avoid presenting experimental schemas as standards.
- Link implementation decisions to public research only when the implementation is intentionally public.

The goal is not to make the repository look busy. The goal is to make every meaningful change easier to inspect, reproduce, and challenge.
