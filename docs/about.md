# About KARADAVI

## The Knowledge Forest

KARADAVI is a knowledge platform for exploring the people, companies, technologies, sciences, histories, places, ideas, natural systems, and cultures that shape the world — together with the relationships between them.

The central idea is simple:

> Knowledge is easier to understand when its connections remain visible.

The internet already contains enormous amounts of information. KARADAVI is not based on the assumption that the world needs another pile of pages. It asks how information can be organized so that identity, context, evidence, provenance, uncertainty, and relationships survive the journey from source to reader.

## From pages to trails

Traditional information systems often make the page the destination.

KARADAVI treats a page as an entrance.

A person may connect to a company. The company may connect to a technology. The technology may connect to a scientific idea. That idea may connect to a historical event, place, institution, or cultural consequence.

Those paths are knowledge trails.

The forest metaphor matters because no useful forest is understood by looking at one tree in isolation.

## What KARADAVI values

KARADAVI favors connected knowledge over isolated facts, inspectable provenance over opaque assertions, visible uncertainty over false precision, and human editorial judgment over automatic publication.

The goal is not to tell readers that every question has one simple answer. The goal is to represent enough context that people can understand what is known, why it is represented that way, and where meaningful connections lead next.

## For humans and machines

Knowledge should be readable by people without becoming useless to software.

KARADAVI therefore explores both editorial presentation and structured representations of entities, claims, evidence, provenance, relationships, authority, uncertainty, and evaluation.

The public specifications in this repository are experimental attempts to make those ideas inspectable.

## What KARADAVI is not

KARADAVI is not a generic chatbot, a universal truth machine, a popularity ranking, a single trust score, or an automatically generated encyclopedia.

It is an attempt to build a quieter, more connected way to explore knowledge.

> **Every page is a starting point. Every connection leads somewhere.**
