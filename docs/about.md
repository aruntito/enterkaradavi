# About KARADAVI

## The Knowledge Forest

KARADAVI is an open knowledge platform being developed as a continuously evolving knowledge institution. Its mission is to make meaningful, identifiable knowledge across the world explorable as an interconnected whole.

It is built around a simple premise:

> Knowledge becomes more understandable when identity, evidence, context and connections remain visible.

The internet contains extraordinary amounts of information, but that information is fragmented across publications, databases, archives, registries and research sources. Entities are duplicated or ambiguously named. Claims conflict. Provenance is lost as information moves between systems. Knowledge becomes stale.

KARADAVI aims to provide a maintained, entity-centered knowledge resource rather than another disconnected collection of pages.

## The north star and the launch boundary

The long-term mission is broad: support the explanation and exploration of meaningful knowledge across human understanding. This is an architectural north star, not a claim that everything can be covered at launch.

The initial product is built around a practical golden path: identify a canonical entity, gather and reconcile source material, preserve evidence and provenance, prepare a structured representation, obtain human editorial approval, and publish an explorable entity page with meaningful connections.

## Ten trails, one extensible knowledge model

The initial discovery experience uses ten knowledge trails:

1. People
2. Companies
3. Technology
4. Science
5. Space
6. Concepts
7. History
8. Places
9. Nature & Earth
10. Society & Culture

These are discovery surfaces, not a closed ontology. The underlying model must support additional entity types, subtypes, claims and relationships without requiring the ten trails to be redesigned every time the scope of knowledge expands.

A person may connect to organizations, technologies, scientific ideas, places, events and cultural contexts. A trail can cross every domain.

## Entity-centered and knowledge-graph-first

An entity is a distinct subject that can be identified, described, connected and revised over time.

KARADAVI separates:

- **Entity:** the subject being represented;
- **Claim:** a proposition about an entity, event or relationship;
- **Source:** where material was obtained;
- **Evidence:** material supporting, weakening or contextualizing a claim;
- **Provenance:** origin and transformation history;
- **Relationship:** a meaningful connection between entities;
- **Authority:** contextual standing to establish a particular kind of claim;
- **Uncertainty:** what remains unknown, ambiguous, incomplete, stale or disputed;
- **Revision:** how a representation changes as evidence and editorial decisions change.

A page is a reader-facing representation of this connected state, not the entire knowledge model.

## Continuous knowledge stewardship

KARADAVI is intended to evolve as the world and its sources change. Its planned stewardship model includes source discovery, research, extraction, normalization, entity resolution, source reconciliation, evidence evaluation, editorial review, publication and later update proposals.

Autonomous discovery and update workflows may identify new subjects, missing relationships, conflicting claims or stale information. They can prepare work for editors; they cannot publish it.

## For humans and machines

Knowledge should be understandable to readers and useful to software. KARADAVI therefore explores human-readable explanations alongside structured representations of entities, claims, evidence, provenance, relationships, authority, uncertainty and revisions.

The public specifications in this repository are experimental research artefacts. They are intended to invite inspection and discussion, not to claim that every semantic question has been solved.

## Editorial invariant

> **AI never publishes.**

AI may research, extract, normalize, classify, compare, draft and propose updates. Human editorial review and explicit approval remain mandatory before publication.

## What KARADAVI is not

KARADAVI is not a generic chatbot, a universal truth machine, a popularity-ranking engine, a single trust score, or an automatically published encyclopedia.

It is an attempt to build a continuously maintained knowledge institution in which readers can explore not only what is represented, but also where it came from, how it is connected and what remains uncertain.

> **Every page is a starting point. Every connection leads somewhere.**
