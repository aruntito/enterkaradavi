# Public Surface

`enterkaradavi` is the public research surface of KARADAVI.

## Intended audience

- engineers building intelligent systems;
- researchers studying machine-readable trust;
- developers working with provenance and knowledge representation;
- contributors interested in semantic infrastructure.

## What belongs here

- public architecture;
- terminology and design principles;
- experimental specifications;
- synthetic or appropriately public examples;
- reproducible research;
- limitations and negative results;
- contribution and security guidance.

## What does not belong here

- credentials or secrets;
- private datasets or personal data;
- private deployment topology;
- internal endpoints;
- proprietary implementation;
- unreleased product work;
- operational details whose publication would weaken the private system.

## Publication test

Before publishing an artifact, ask:

> Does this make the public research more understandable or reproducible without exposing private implementation or operational knowledge?

If the answer is unclear, keep it private and review it before publication.
